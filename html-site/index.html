Q: What is the cost of hiring a Bay Area DJ?

The cost of hiring a DJ in the Bay Area varies based on factors like the DJ’s experience, type of event, location, parking, and included services. Here’s a general breakdown:

  • San Francisco Average DJ Price: $999
  • San Jose Average DJ Price: $800
  • Budget DJs: Starting at $200
  • High-End DJs: Up to $20,000
  • Weddings with MC: $200 – $5,000+ per hour
  • Private Parties: $100 – $750+ per hour
  • Corporate Events: $200 – $1,000+ per hour

Additional costs may apply for:

  • Lighting, karaoke, photo booth, 360 photo booth
  • Cold sparklers, “dancing on the cloud”, LED dance floors
  • Battery-powered speakers, generators, and other rentals
  • Stairs, beach setups, long-distance travel
  • Bilingual or multilingual DJs
