ACTIVITIES AND GAMES
*Sing part of a song, give a toast, or tell a story to get the bride and groom to kiss
*Sing part of a song to be allowed to go to the buffet table
*Blindfold the guy who caught garter before he removes the garter from the leg of the lady who caught the bouquet
*Married couples dance and we find out who has been married the longest at the reception
*Blindfold the bride and she guesses which arm muscle belongs to the groom
*Blindfold the groom and he feels hands or calves and guesses which one belongs to the bride
*The bride moves an egg through the grooms pants
*The bride and groom eat something and come together for a kiss
*The groom pays the bride a compliment for each step he takes toward her
*The bride is blindfolded and has to find the money on the groom
*Guests have to do what the bride and groom say to get a personal item back
*”Ex-boyfriends” and “ex-girlfriends” return the “keys” to the bride and groom’s apartments
*Musical chairs
*The groom guesses which lipstick mark belongs to the bride
*The groom is blindfolded and has to walk without knocking down the wine bottles
*The bride has to catch the cherry
*Guests need to get pencils, which are hanging on strings behind them, in the bottles
