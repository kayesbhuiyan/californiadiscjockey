]]> Sir Francis Drake Hotel, San Francisco … Wedding DJ Testimonial.

We hired Chris to play our wedding at the Sir Francis Drake Hotel.  We stressed out because we wanted the event to be perfect and it really was.

During the evening we asked Chris to move from the Ballroom to the mezzanine and back and up to the Starlight room.  He had the best attitude even though we drove him crazy with special requests.

We gave him ideas of what we’d like to hear and what he didn’t have he was able to track down for us.  His mixing was impeccable, no awkward moments and he really was engaging.  His emceeing deserves a special achievement award and with half of our wedding party being Thai, he worked so hard announcing  the 7 last syllable names.

We loved the fact that he was a one stop shop for other items besides music, offering rentals without pressure.  We went with his photo booth and opted for a video presentation.  The evening was a success.  All in all, personal,  professional before, during and after.  Our guests had a great time down to the very end. I’d have no hesitation about referring him to anyone.

Bruce Z, Tiburon, California
